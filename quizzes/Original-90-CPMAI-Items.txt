QUESTION 1  [Identify Business Needs and Solutions]  (used in Mock Exam 2)
------------------------------------------------------------
A hospital executive asks for an AI solution to 'improve operations.' Bed management, nurse staffing, and coding denials are all mentioned in the same steering meeting, and each vice president believes their issue is the priority.

What should the AI project manager do first?

  A. Commission a time-boxed proof of concept for each proposed use case so leadership can compare early results.
  B. Facilitate structured stakeholder interviews to define the problem, personas, and intended outcomes before selecting a use case.
  C. Inventory available EHR tables and labeling capacity so technical feasibility can bound the discussion.
  D. Draft a combined scope that includes bed management, staffing, and denials to preserve executive alignment.

ANSWER: B

Why this is correct:
When the problem itself is unsettled, the first action is to identify and validate the need, personas, and outcomes. Proofs of concept, data inventories, and combined scope all assume a problem that has not yet been agreed.

Why other options are incorrect:
  A: Parallel POCs start solution work before the organization has aligned on which problem to solve, which spreads effort across unvalidated directions.
  C: Data inventory supports feasibility later; it does not resolve competing problem statements.
  D: Combining every executive request into one scope avoids conflict but produces an unfocused initiative.


QUESTION 2  [Identify Business Needs and Solutions]  (used in Mock Exam 2)
------------------------------------------------------------
A bank proposes an NLP classifier for complaint routing. A rules engine already meets the service-level agreement, but the analytics lead argues that NLP will 'future-proof' the process. Historical complaint volume is stable.

What should the AI project manager do when evaluating initial feasibility?

  A. Compare the proposed AI approach with the existing rules-based process against cost, risk, performance, and maintainability.
  B. Recommend replacing the rules engine in the first release to avoid operating two routing mechanisms.
  C. Proceed to model selection because complaint text is already stored in the case system.
  D. Approve a limited NLP pilot because language tasks are a standard AI pattern.

ANSWER: A

Why this is correct:
Feasibility includes whether AI is warranted versus a working traditional alternative. Pattern popularity, a dual-system cleanup, or data availability do not by themselves justify the change.

Why other options are incorrect:
  D: Treating NLP as automatically appropriate skips the AI-versus-traditional comparison the scenario requires.
  B: Replacing a process that already meets SLAs commits to AI before feasibility is established.
  C: Available text is necessary but not sufficient to show that a learning system is the right solution.
