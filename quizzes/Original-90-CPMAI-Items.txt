QUESTION 1  [Identify Business Needs and Solutions]  (used in Mock Exam 2)
------------------------------------------------------------
A hospital wants an AI tool that 'makes operations better.' Executives cannot agree whether the pain point is bed turnover, staffing, or coding denials.

What should the AI project manager do first?

  A. Select a computer vision vendor that other hospitals have used for bed tracking.
  B. Facilitate stakeholder interviews to define the problem, personas, and success outcomes.
  C. Commission a proof of concept on all three pain points in parallel.
  D. Request a data lake refresh so modeling can start while the problem is clarified.

ANSWER: B

Why this is correct:
AI work starts with a validated problem and personas. Interviews convert a vague request into a specific need before vendors, POCs, or data work begin.

Why other options are incorrect:
  A, C: Vendor selection and parallel POCs lock in solutions before the organization agrees on the problem to solve.
  D: Refreshing data infrastructure does not define the business problem and can waste effort on the wrong use case.

A new sector AI rule will take effect before the planned launch. The team has not read it.

What should the AI project manager do for regulatory monitoring?

  A. Launch first and retrofit if fined.
  B. Track the requirement, engage legal/compliance, and adjust the plan and controls before launch.
  C. Assume general GDPR knowledge covers every sector rule.
  D. Assign the intern to 'watch Twitter for regs'.

ANSWER: B

Why this is correct:
Compliance monitoring is active: track rules, coordinate with legal, implement controls, document.

Why other options are incorrect:
  A, C, D: Launch-first, over-generalizing, or informal social tracking is not a compliance process.

QUESTION 90  [Support Responsible and Trustworthy AI]  (used in Mock Exam 3)
------------------------------------------------------------
During bias testing, a small demographic group has too few samples for a stable fairness metric.

What should the AI project manager do?

  A. Ignore the group because the metric is noisy.
  B. Treat small-n as a data representation risk, report uncertainty, and seek more data or constrained deployment.
  C. Duplicate the few rows until the p-value looks good.
  D. Exclude the group from the product silently.

ANSWER: B

Why this is correct:
Sparse groups are a representation problem. Report uncertainty and mitigate; do not fake stats or silently exclude.

Why other options are incorrect:
  A, C, D: Ignoring, duplicating, or silent exclusion are unethical or invalid.
