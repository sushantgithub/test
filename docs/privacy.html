Privacy Policy for CPMAI Study

Last updated: 28 August 2026

CPMAI Study is an offline exam-study app. It does not require an account and does not include ads or analytics SDKs.

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Study progress (topics you mark as studied and quiz self-scores) is stored only on your device using Android app preferences. Uninstalling the app deletes that information.

Internet

The app does not require internet permission and does not send your notes or scores to a server.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a professional exam. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
